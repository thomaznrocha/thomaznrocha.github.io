Thomaz Novais Rocha

Postdoctoral Researcher

Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile · Santiago, Chile

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile and hold a PhD in Business Administration from FGV EAESP, Brazil. My research lies at the intersection of social entrepreneurship, business ethics, and entrepreneurship, with particular interest in how entrepreneurs navigate ethical tensions, develop resources and capabilities, and create social value. My current research examines the interplay between ethical decision-making and social value creation in social enterprises, building on my doctoral research on how social entrepreneurs develop intangible capitals through social impact accelerators.
