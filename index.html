Thomaz Novais Rocha

Postdoctoral Researcher, Institute for Applied Ethics

Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, Santiago, Chile

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute for Applied Ethics at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile and hold a PhD in Business Administration from FGV EAESP, Brazil. My research focuses on social entrepreneurship, entrepreneurial behavior, entrepreneurial support, and business ethics. I am particularly interested in how entrepreneurs navigate tensions and develop resources and capabilities, as well as in the role of support organizations and entrepreneurial ecosystems in shaping these processes.

Before entering academia, I served as a Technical Advisor at the Secretariat of Economic Development of the State of Ceará, Brazil, where I worked on business attraction, innovation, and regional development initiatives. I have also worked as a consultant and advisor to social ventures and contributed to initiatives connecting entrepreneurship, innovation, and social impact.

Outside academia, I love photography, listen to a lot of progressive rock, and occasionally try to play some of it on the guitar. I am also a dog lover, and some of my wildest research ideas tend to emerge during walks with my dog, Mika.
