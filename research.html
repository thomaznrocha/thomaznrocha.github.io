Research

My research focuses on social entrepreneurship and entrepreneurial behavior, with particular attention to ethical decision-making, social value creation, and the role of support organizations and ecosystems in shaping entrepreneurs and ventures.

The interplay between social entrepreneurs’ ethical decision-making and social value creation

with Ignacio Pavez and Alejandra Marinovic

Postdoctoral research project examining the relationship between ethical decision-making and social value creation in social enterprises.

Presented at Academy of Management Andes Connect Workshop 2025 · Preparing submission to the Journal of Business Ethics.

Developing Intangible Capitals in Social Entrepreneurship: The Role of Social Impact Accelerators

with Maria Tereza Fleury, Edgard Barki, and José Milton de Sousa-Filho

Research on how social entrepreneurs develop intangible capitals through social impact accelerators.

Presented at BALAS 2024 and ENANPAD 2024 · Under review at the Journal of Management Studies.

The weight of doing good: startup accelerators and founder well-being in social entrepreneurship

with Dianne M. Neutzling

Research examining accelerators and founder well-being in the context of social entrepreneurship.

Presented at ENANPAD 2026 · Nominated for Best Paper in the Entrepreneurship Track · Preparing submission to the Journal of Business Venturing.

How social entrepreneurs build and balance social and commercial discourses in pitching presentations

Research on how social entrepreneurs construct and balance social and commercial discourses in entrepreneurial pitches.
